DashFactor – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 November 2026 · Published by Zynora Technologies, Pietermaritzburg, KwaZulu-Natal, South Africa

DashFactor ("the game") is a free maths runner game for mobile devices, made by Zynora Technologies ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information is collected when you play, why, and the choices you have.

1. Who the game is for

DashFactor is intended for players aged 13 and older. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the game and you want something removed, contact us (section 9).

2. Information we collect ourselves

None that identifies you. The game has no accounts, no sign-in and no chat. Your progress (high score, unlocked worlds, sound setting and a count of how many times you have opened the game) is stored only on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the game. We do not receive it.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free because it shows ads, served by Google AdMob. Some ads are optional "rewarded" ads you choose to watch for an in-game benefit (for example, continuing a run). When an ad is requested, Google may collect and process the following from your device:

InformationUsed for
Device advertising ID (Android Advertising ID / Apple IDFA where you allow it)Showing ads, limiting how often you see the same ad, measuring ad performance, preventing fraud
IP address and approximate location derived from itChoosing ads relevant to your country, fraud prevention
Interactions with ads (views, taps)Ad delivery, reporting and payment to us
Device and app information (model, operating system, app version) and crash diagnosticsAd delivery and making ads work correctly on your device

Google acts under its own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. How Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Ads shown are limited to content suitable for teenagers.

4. Your choices about ads

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland): the first time you open the game you are asked whether ads may be personalised. You can change your choice at any time with the PRIVACY button on the main menu.
  • Android: Settings > Privacy > Ads lets you reset or delete your advertising ID.
  • iPhone/iPad: if asked, you can decline tracking; you can change this in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • If you opt out of personalised ads you will still see ads, but they will be less relevant.

5. Sharing and selling

We do not sell personal information. The only third party receiving data from the game is Google, for the advertising purposes above. Data sent to Google is encrypted in transit.

6. Retention and deletion

Data stored on your device is removed when you uninstall the game. We hold no player data on our own servers. Data held by Google is kept according to Google's policies; you can manage it through your Google account and your device's ad settings.

7. Your rights (POPIA, GDPR and similar laws)

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information, to object to processing, and to complain to a regulator. In South Africa this is the Information Regulator (inforegulator.org.za). Because we do not hold player data ourselves, most requests are handled through the ad choices in section 4 or directly with Google, but we will help if you contact us.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes (for example, adding analytics or in-app purchases) will be reflected here before they reach the game.
